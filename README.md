## 🧠 Study Overview

This study investigated neurotransmitter levels in the **periaqueductal gray (PAG)** of participants with **chronic low back pain** compared to pain-free controls using magnetic resonance spectroscopy (MRS), and whether neurotransmitter levels relate to psychophysical measures of descending pain modulation and pain sensitivity. 
The dataset includes [n=40] participants with chronic low back pain and [n=28] pain-free controls. 

**PLEASE NOTE**: One participant with chronic low back pain and one pain-free control did not consent for further use of their data and thus, their data was removed from the dataset.
